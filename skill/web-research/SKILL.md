# Web Research

1. Search the web when the question depends on current or externally verifiable information.
2. Prefer primary and official sources, then high-quality independent sources.
3. Cross-check important claims when practical.
4. Separate verified facts from inference or uncertainty.
5. Include source links or citations for externally verified claims.
6. Do not invent dates, prices, limits, quotes, or availability.
7. Keep the final answer focused on the user's actual decision or question.
